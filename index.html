FAQs

Before You Call, Check Here

Answers to the questions Wallaceburg homeowners ask us most.

Are your technicians certified and insured?

Both, on every job, with documentation available on request. There is no Ontario trade licence for duct cleaning, so training, certification, and insurance are the standards that separate real crews, and all three apply here.

Our wartime house had ducts added decades after it was built. Any issue?

None, as long as the crew knows what they are looking at. A good share of Wallaceburg's worker cottages got their ductwork long after the walls went up, with runs that make creative turns and boots that were never quite sealed. We walk the layout first and brush accordingly, gently wherever the metal is original.

How often should a Wallaceburg home be cleaned?

Homes near the channels or over damp basements do best every two years, since humidity keeps the dust heavy and the must persistent. Drier, newer builds out toward the edges can stretch toward three or four.

What signals a clogged dryer vent here?

Drying times stretching past one cycle, heat building in the laundry room, lint visible at the hood, or the machine shutting off mid-load. Near the water, add nests and insects at the cap to the usual lint.

Can you clear the musty smell from our vents?

Normally yes. Cleaning carries out the damp residue that holds the odour, and a sanitizer applied to the clean duct walls addresses what lingers. In river-side homes the pairing makes the difference through the next humid fall.

Why does the house dust no matter how much we clean?

Retrofit ductwork with suction-side leaks keeps refeeding the system from the basement, a near-universal issue in this housing stock. A leak test finds it, and sealing ends the cycle.

Do you service the seasonal cabins and places along the channels?

Yes. Properties out along the Sydenham channels and the seasonal cabins among them get the same whole-system cleaning scaled to smaller systems, and spring is the natural window before the humidity lands. Access by lane or by water just needs a note at booking so we bring the right setup.

Do you serve the communities around Wallaceburg?

Yes, including Dresden, Tupperville, Sombra, Port Lambton, Bothwell, and Chatham, plus the rural routes and channel properties between them.
